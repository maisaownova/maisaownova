### A little about me

I'm studying Informatics at Masaryk University and
currently working as a Junior Data Engineer at InfoTech-CS.

I enjoy programming, but I also have a soft spot for
design and branding. I love taking an idea and turning
it into something real, especially when I get to work
with people and bring their vision to life.

When I'm building a website for someone, I don't want
it to look like just another template. I want it to feel
like *them* — their personality, their business, their
little details. That's probably my favorite part of
working on frontend projects.

On the more technical side, I work with data pipelines,
databases and software development. I genuinely enjoy
both sides, even if they seem quite different.
